Algorithmic Physics

How SachScan Detects What Humans Miss

Modern generative models fool the human eye and traditional optical filters. SachScan probes the underlying mathematical physics of how digital sensors capture photons versus neural networks synthesizing tokens.

01 account_tree

Claim DNA — Break one forward into claims

A single message may contain three claims: one true, one misleading, and one fabricated. Extract and verify each separately instead of assigning one verdict to the entire message.

> Example: “A new government scheme gives ₹50,000 to every student.” The system separately checks whether the scheme exists, who qualifies, and whether the amount is correct.
02 fact_check

Evidence Lens — Show, don't just tell

Highlight the exact passage from an official notice, government website, or reputable report that supports or contradicts each claim. Display the publication date, source, and what the evidence cannot establish.

> Unique value: users can inspect the proof themselves rather than trusting an AI-generated answer.
03 image_search

Context & Media Detective

Check whether an image or video is old, reused, cropped, or presented with a misleading caption. Inspect available metadata and use reverse-image search where available.

> Important: detecting edits or finding an older copy does not automatically prove a message is fake. The assistant should state uncertainty honestly.
04 quickreply

Share-Safe Reply — Stop misinformation at the source

Generate a short, respectful reply in the original language that users can share with the person or group that sent the forward.

> Example: “This message isn't confirmed by the official sources checked. Please verify the announcement before forwarding it further.”
